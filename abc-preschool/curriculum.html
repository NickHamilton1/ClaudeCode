Curriculum

Bible-based, hands-on, and built for little learners.

ABC Preschool uses the ABeka curriculum — a Christian, Biblically-based program focused on academic excellence and character training. At the preschool level, that means songs, stories, letter and number recognition, Bible memory, and the kind of hands-on play that helps young children love learning.

What ABeka looks like at preschool age

You may have read mixed reviews of ABeka online. Most of them are about the upper-grade workbooks, not the preschool program. At ages 2, 3, and 4, ABeka is much more about singing, listening, and discovering than worksheets.

A typical morning includes Bible story time, songs and finger-plays, hands-on letter and number activities, art and craft projects, free play in centers, and outdoor time. Children are introduced to academic concepts in age-appropriate ways — and never pushed past where they're developmentally ready.

Why we chose ABeka

It puts Scripture at the center of everything — from memory verses to lessons from the Old Testament and Life of Christ — while still preparing children well for kindergarten. That combination of faith and academic readiness is what we're called to provide.

Specials that round out the day

In addition to the ABeka curriculum, every age group enjoys regular "specials":

  • Chapel Service — age-appropriate worship and Bible teaching
  • Music — songs, rhythm, and movement
  • Spanish & Sign Language — exposure to a second language

Children also enjoy library visits, professional presentations at the preschool, and external field trips throughout the year.

Scope & Sequence
What your child will learn, by age

Two-Year-Olds

Building blocks of language & number

Language Arts

  • Language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)

Numbers

  • Simple counting 1–25
  • Number concepts 1–10

Bible

  • Memory verses
  • Lessons from the Old Testament and the Life of Christ

Music, Arts & Crafts

  • Songs, rhythm, and creative play

Three-Year-Olds

Letters, sounds, and growing independence

Language Arts

  • Recognition of name, sound, and picture for short vowels and consonants
  • Formation of vowels and consonants in upper and lower case
  • Language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)

Numbers

  • Simple counting 1–30
  • Number concepts 1–15

Bible

  • Memory verses
  • Lessons from the Old Testament and the Life of Christ

Music, Arts & Crafts

  • Colors, shapes, following directions, eye-hand coordination

Four-Year-Olds

Reading, writing, and getting ready for kindergarten

Language Arts — Phonics & Reading

  • Recognition of name, sound, and picture of long and short vowels and consonants
  • Sounding of blends and one- and two-vowel words
  • Reading of sentences and stories with one- and two-vowel words

Writing

  • Formation of letters, blends, and words
  • Writing of first name

Language

  • Poems and finger plays committed to memory
  • Language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)

Numbers

  • Number recognition and counting 1–100
  • Number concepts 1–20
  • Numbers before and after 1–20
  • Numbers largest and smallest 1–20
  • Addition facts (1+1, 2+1, 3+1, etc.)

Bible

  • Memory verses
  • Lessons from the Old Testament and the Life of Christ

Music, Arts & Crafts

  • Development of motor skills in drawing, coloring, cutting, and gluing
