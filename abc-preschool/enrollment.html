What's included

  • Full ABeka curriculum and supporting materials
  • Weekly chapel, music, and Spanish/Sign Language specials
  • Library visits, professional presentations, and external field trips
  • All classroom supplies and craft materials
Honest pricing

Tuition is paid monthly. The $100 registration fee is a one-time, non-refundable charge due at the time you enroll. There are no surprise add-ons after registration.

For comparison

Care.com data lists full-day daycare in Concord at $600–$1,500 per month. ABC's half-day program at $290–$385 reflects what a Christian church preschool can offer when it's run as a ministry rather than a business — academic and faith formation at less than half the cost of full-day childcare.

How to apply

Applications are accepted year-round, with priority given to families who apply earlier in the calendar year. Most classrooms fill by spring for the following school year.

Step 1 — Schedule a tour

Come see the classrooms, meet Korighan, and get a feel for the rhythm of a typical morning. Tours are the best way to know whether ABC is the right fit for your family. Email Korighan or call 704-786-9167 to schedule.

Step 2 — Submit an application

After your tour, Korighan will provide an application packet. The packet includes a brief enrollment form and a media release form. Both can be returned by email or in person.

Step 3 — Pay the registration fee

Once your application is reviewed, the $100 registration fee secures your child's spot. Tuition begins the first month of attendance.

Forms

The Preschool Media Release Form is available here as a PDF. Korighan will provide the full enrollment application after your tour.

Have questions before you apply?

The most common questions about membership, faith, ratios, drop-off, and what to bring are answered on our FAQ page. For anything not covered there, call or email — Korighan answers personally.

Ready to start the conversation?

The fastest way to find out if ABC is the right fit is to come see it. Tours take 20–30 minutes and you're welcome to bring your child along.
