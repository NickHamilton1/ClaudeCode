FAQ

The questions families ask most often.

Honest answers about church membership, faith, daily life in the classroom, and the practical stuff every parent wants to know before applying. If you don't see your question here, call or email Korighan — she answers personally.

About ABC & FBC Concord

Do we have to be members of First Baptist Church Concord to enroll?

No. ABC Preschool is a ministry of First Baptist Church Concord, but church membership is not required to enroll your child. Families from across Concord and Cabarrus County are welcome.

Are non-Christian families welcome?

Yes — and we mean it. Families of any background are welcome to apply. We're honest with prospective families about what your child will hear and learn at ABC: Bible stories at circle time, prayer at meals and chapel, and faith woven into songs and lessons. No child is ever singled out for not knowing a verse or prayer. The goal is for every child to feel loved and at home in the classroom.

If you're weighing whether ABC is the right environment for your family, we'd rather you know exactly what to expect than feel surprised later. Call or email Korighan and she'll answer any specific questions honestly.

How much Bible is in the day?

Bible is woven through the day, not concentrated in one block. A typical morning includes a Bible story at circle time, prayer at meals, and Bible-based songs and memory verses as part of the ABeka curriculum. Every age group also attends regular chapel as part of their "specials" rotation.

For a fuller sense of the rhythm, see the Curriculum page.

What does "All Because of Christ" mean?

That's what ABC stands for. Everything we do in the classroom — from teaching letters to handling conflicts — flows from the conviction that children are loved, known, and made on purpose by God. The name reminds us why we exist.

The Classroom & Daily Life

What are the hours? Is there extended care?

ABC runs Monday through Friday, 8:45 am – 12:30 pm, during the school year. We're a half-day preschool, so we don't currently offer before- or after-care. Drop-off begins at 8:45 and pickup is at 12:30 sharp.

What's the teacher-to-child ratio?

Ratios vary by age group, with the youngest classrooms staffed most heavily. Korighan can share the specific ratio for the class your child would be in — please call or email for the current numbers.

What does my child need to bring each day?

For all ages: a backpack with a labeled change of clothes, a refillable water bottle, and any comfort items (a small blanket or stuffed animal) your child might want. ABC provides all classroom supplies, craft materials, and curriculum. Specifics — including snack guidelines and what to send for younger classrooms — are covered on your tour and in the enrollment packet.

What's your discipline philosophy?

Gentle, consistent, and tied to the heart — not just the behavior. We use redirection, clear and simple expectations, and age-appropriate consequences (like a brief break from an activity). We work closely with parents on anything ongoing so home and school stay aligned. The goal is always for a child to feel known and loved while learning to make good choices.

How is drop-off and separation handled?

We've walked many first-time families through this. Most kids settle within the first week or two, even when drop-off is rough at the start. Our teachers have years of experience helping children transition, and we'll communicate with you throughout the day if needed. The first morning is the hardest — it gets easier from there.

Curriculum & ABeka

Is ABeka really academically rigorous? I've read mixed reviews.

Most of the strong opinions about ABeka online come from the upper-grade workbook program — not preschool. At ages 2, 3, and 4, ABeka is built around songs, stories, hands-on activities, Bible memory, and age-appropriate letter and number recognition. There's no testing, no homework, and no screen time. Children are met where they are developmentally.

The Curriculum page walks through what each age group covers in detail.

Is there homework or testing at this age?

No. There is no homework and no formal testing at any preschool age. Teachers track each child's progress informally to know how to support them — but parents aren't expected to do "school" at home.

Is my child ready for preschool?

Most children are ready when they can spend a few hours away from a parent and follow simple routines. They don't need to know letters, numbers, colors, or shapes — that's what we're here to teach. Emotional readiness and basic independence (like trying new things, accepting comfort from a teacher) matter more than any pre-academic skill.

If you're unsure, schedule a tour. Korighan has a good eye for whether a child is ready and which classroom would be the best fit.

Calendar, Tuition & Logistics

Do you follow Cabarrus County Schools' calendar?

Not exactly. ABC Preschool sets its own calendar and does not directly follow the Cabarrus County School System schedule. Inclement weather decisions are made by the ABC director on a per-situation basis. The full school-year calendar is published on the Calendar page.

What does tuition cost? Are there hidden fees?

For 2026–2027, tuition is $290/month for 3 days (MWF) and $385/month for 5 days (M–F). There's a one-time, non-refundable $100 registration fee due when you enroll. No hidden fees or surprise add-ons. Full details are on the Tuition & Apply page.

How does the application process work?

The simplest path is: schedule a tour, fill out the enrollment forms Korighan provides, and pay the $100 registration fee to secure your spot. Applications are accepted year-round, but most classes fill by spring for the following school year, so applying earlier is better.

Do you have a waitlist?

It depends on the age group and time of year. Some classrooms have openings; others fill quickly and operate with a waitlist. Call or email Korighan with the age and start year you're considering and she'll let you know honestly where things stand.

Is there a sibling discount?

Please ask Korighan about current discount and assistance options when you tour — these can change year to year.
