Our Mission

Worship in Spirit & Truth

The Music & Worship Ministry of First Baptist Church exists for the Glory of God and to promote Worship in Spirit and Truth (John 4:24) and Singing in the Spirit and with Understanding (1 Corinthians 14:15).

Regardless of whether you play an instrument or sing, come and let us praise the Triune God with our talents in all areas of music. There's a place here for every voice and every skill level.

Ways to Participate

Four different groups lead worship throughout the year — each with its own rehearsal time and open to new members.

Adult Choir

Wed 7:30–8:30 PM · Choir Room

Provides musical leadership in the 10:45 AM Sunday worship service. Rehearsals are held weekly in the choir room.

Praise Team

Sunday Services

Helps lead music for Sunday morning services with contemporary worship songs alongside the choir and orchestra.

Church Orchestra

Sun 9:30 AM · Sanctuary

Provides musical leadership in the 10:45 AM service. Open to anyone in 9th grade or above who plays an instrument.

Handbells

Wed 6:30 PM · Music Suite

Adult handbells play for morning worship quarterly and practice in Fall and Spring. They also attend an annual handbell conference.
