Meet Our Staff

The people who serve and lead at First Baptist Church Concord. We'd love to connect with you.

Leadership

Pastoral Team

Dr. Jim Collier
Senior Pastor

Jim is a Florida native and spent 12 years serving our country in the US Army, attaining the rank of Captain before entering seminary. He attended Mid America Baptist Seminary for his M.Div and Doctorate. Jim and his wife Myra have been married almost 30 years and have 2 adult children.

Email Pastor Jim
Aaron Edwards
Associate Pastor to Youth & Families

Aaron grew up in Mount Pleasant, NC. He is a graduate of Lee University, Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary, and is currently in the D.Min. program at Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary. He has been serving the youth of FBC Concord since August 2013. He and his wife Amanda reside in Kannapolis.

Email Pastor Aaron
Luke Waters
Worship Pastor

Born in Advance NC but raised in Concord, Luke is the son of a local pastor. He is a graduate of Northwest High, North Greenville University, and Southwestern Theological Seminary (MDiv). Luke has a talent and passion for music and using it to praise the Lord. He and his wife Katherine make their home in Salisbury.
