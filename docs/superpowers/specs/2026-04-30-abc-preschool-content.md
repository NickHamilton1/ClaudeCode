## Mission statement

ABC Preschool is a ministry of First Baptist Church of Concord. We hope to serve our community by serving our young children in weekday early education. It is our desire to provide a safe and loving Christian environment where children can grow spiritually, intellectually, and socially.

## Curriculum

All Because of Christ uses the **ABeka curriculum** — a well-known Christian, Biblically-based curriculum that focuses on academic excellence and character training.

### Curriculum overview by age

#### Two-year-olds
- **Language Arts** — Language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)
- **Numbers** — Simple counting 1–25; number concepts 1–10
- **Bible** — Memory verses; lessons from Old Testament and Life of Christ
- **Music, Arts & Crafts**

#### Three-year-olds
- **Language Arts** — Recognition of name, sound, and picture for short vowels and consonants; formation of vowels and consonants in upper and lower case; language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)
- **Numbers** — Simple counting 1–30; number concepts 1–15
- **Bible** — Memory verses; lessons from Old Testament and Life of Christ
- **Music, Arts & Crafts** — Colors, shapes, following directions, eye-hand coordination

#### Four-year-olds
- **Language Arts — Phonics and Reading** — Recognition of name, sound, and picture of long and short vowels and consonants; sounding of blends and one- and two-vowel words; reading of sentences and stories with one- and two-vowel words
- **Writing** — Formation of letters, blends, and words; writing of first name
- **Language** — Poems and finger plays committed to memory; language development and listening skills (color words, shapes, animals, transportation, countries, community helpers, health, safety, manners, science)
- **Numbers** — Number recognition and counting 1–100; number concepts 1–20; numbers before and after 1–20; numbers largest and smallest 1–20; addition facts (1+1, 2+1, 3+1, etc.)
- **Bible** — Memory verses; lessons from Old Testament and Life of Christ
- **Music, Arts & Crafts** — Development of motor skills in drawing, coloring, cutting, and gluing
