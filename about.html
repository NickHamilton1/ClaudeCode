Who We Are

About Our Church

First Baptist Church Concord is a family of imperfect people connecting with our perfect Lord Jesus through worship, discipleship, and ministry in our community.

Loving God. Loving Others.
Making Disciples.

We're a Southern Baptist church in the heart of Concord, North Carolina, rooted in Scripture and committed to sharing the gospel of Jesus Christ. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church home, you're welcome here — just as you are.

Loving God

We gather weekly to worship the Lord through Scripture, song, and prayer — giving Him our hearts, minds, and lives in gratitude for His grace.

Loving Others

We believe the church is a family. We care for one another, serve our neighbors, and welcome everyone regardless of background or stage of life.

Making Disciples

From Concord to the ends of the earth, we train and send believers to share the good news of Jesus, following His call in Matthew 28.

Our Story

Rooted in Concord.
Reaching the world.

First Baptist Church Concord has called 200 Branchview Drive home for generations. What began as a gathering of believers has grown into a church family of all ages — worshiping together every Sunday and serving our community throughout the week.

We're unapologetically missional. As an Acts 1:8 church, we're committed to sharing Christ locally through our neighbors, regionally and nationally through partnerships, and globally through international missions.

What We Believe

Grounded in the gospel

Our beliefs are rooted in Scripture and align with the Baptist Faith and Message. Here are a few of the convictions that shape who we are.

The Bible

The Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God — our final authority for faith and practice. We preach it expositionally each Sunday.

Jesus Christ

Jesus is fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and is the only way to the Father.

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is a gift of God's grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, so no one can boast.

The Church

The local church is God's plan for discipleship, worship, and mission — a family of believers committed to one another and to the world.
