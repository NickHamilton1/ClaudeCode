FAQ

Common Questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. First Baptist Church Concord is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and all gifts are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. You'll receive a year-end giving statement for your records.

Where does my gift go?

Gifts to the General Fund support the everyday ministry of FBC Concord — staff, missions, worship, discipleship, facilities, and outreach. You can also designate gifts to specific funds like Missions, Building & Grounds, Benevolence, or Youth Ministry using the dropdown above.

Is online giving secure?

When our online portal launches, it will be powered by Planning Center, an industry-leading church software platform. All transactions are encrypted and processed through PCI-compliant payment providers. We never store your card or bank information on our servers.

Can I change or cancel a recurring gift?

Absolutely. Once recurring giving is live, you'll be able to update, pause, or cancel your gift at any time through your donor account. In the meantime, contact Cindy Key at ckey@fbcconcord.org for any changes.
