Children’s Ministry

The purpose of the Children’s Ministry of First Baptist Church is to partner with and support parent(s) or guardian(s) of children (birth – grade 6) as they diligently and intentionally lead, disciple, and empower their children with the Word of God so that both discover, develop, and display the greatness of Christ in all of life. In order to fulfill this purpose the Children’s Ministry will provide discipleship, evangelism, fellowship, ministry, and worship opportunities that challenge both the parent(s) and the child to become mature believers in the Christian faith. In John 10:10, Jesus said, ”I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full.” This abundant life is the life that we, at First Baptist, desire for all children. The Children’s Ministry leadership of First Baptist Church stands as a united front teaching children the absolute truths that are found in God’s Word, The Holy Bible and His Son, Jesus Christ.
 
Feel free to contact me, I would love to talk to you about the shepherding of your child’s heart.
