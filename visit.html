Your First Sunday

Plan Your Visit

The best way to find out what FBC Concord is all about is to join us on a Sunday morning. You've already taken the first step by visiting us online — here's everything you need to know for your first visit.

Service Time Sundays at 10:45 AM
Address 200 Branchview Dr SE, Concord, NC
Service Length About 75 minutes
Getting Here

When You Arrive

Sunday morning worship services at FBC are open to all regardless of age, gender, religious belief, disability, handicap, race or ethnicity.

  • Parking

    Enter through the main entrance on Branchview Dr, then drive around to the back of the building. Handicap parking is available near the main entrance with level wheelchair access.

  • Guest Services

    Our friendly ushers will welcome you at the main entrance. The Information Services desk is directly to your left — let them know it's your first time and they'll take care of you from there.

  • Worship Center

    The Worship Center is to the right behind the main entrance. You are welcome to sit in any available seat. If the service has already begun, an usher will help guide you.

  • Accessibility

    We offer several locations in the worship center for wheelchairs. Please see one of our ushers for assistance or stop by the Information Services desk near the worship center entrance.

For Families

Bringing Your Kids?

We offer childcare for ages birth through age 3. Classes for elementary-aged children are available at our 9:30 AM Sunday School, and Kingdom Kidz children's worship is available during our 10:45 AM service for ages 4 through 2nd grade.

When you arrive, visit our Information Services desk and someone will assist you in getting your children checked in to their classes.

Plan on arriving 15 minutes before the service so you won't miss the beginning.

Sunday School at 9:30 AM

Before worship, we offer classes for all ages. It's a great way to connect in a smaller group setting and dig deeper into Scripture.

Kingdom Kidz at 10:45 AM

Ages 4 through 2nd grade enjoy their own worship experience during the main service — with music, Bible stories, and activities made just for them.

Sunday Morning

What to Expect in Worship

Our service lasts about 75 minutes and is a combination of music, Scripture reading, teaching, and prayer.

Music

Choir and orchestra-led worship with selections ranging from hymns to contemporary songs. You're encouraged to stand, sit, raise your hands, and sing your heart out.

Scripture Reading

We believe in hiding God's word in our hearts. Our call to worship opens with Scripture, and we recite Scripture before the sermon.

Preaching

Typically Pastor Jim Collier or Pastor Aaron Edwards will teach expositionally from Scripture. The message is usually around 40 minutes.

Lord's Supper

Every other month on Sunday morning we take the Lord's Supper together. This time is designated specifically for followers of Christ.

Baptism

Once a quarter we celebrate with those who have trusted Christ alone for eternal salvation, recognizing their profession of faith through baptism.

Information Areas

After the service, you can ask questions or speak to the Pastor. Two Information desks are located at the front and rear entrances of the church.
