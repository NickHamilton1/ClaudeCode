Did you know..................that the Music & Worship Ministry Of First Baptist Church exists for the Glory of God and to promote Worship in Spirit & Truth (John 4:24) and Singing in the Spirit & with Understanding (I Corinthians 14:15)?  

 

Here are some opportunities for participation:

 
Adult Choir: Provides musical leadership in the 10:45am worship service. Rehearsals are on Wednesday from 7:30 pm-8:30 pm in the choir room.
 
Praise Team: Helps lead music for Sunday morning services.
 
Church Orchestra: Provides musical leadership in the 10:45 am worship service. Rehearsals are on Sunday morning at 9:30 am in the sanctuary. Membership is open to anyone who plays an instrument and is at least in the 9th grade or above.
 
Handbells: The adult handbells play for morning worship quarterly and practice on Wednesday night at 6:30 in the music suite during the Fall and Spring. They attend a handbell conference yearly as well as playing locally for concerts and other groups.
