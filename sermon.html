The Heart of Worship

Pastor Jim Collier
Romans 12 Worship Spiritual Growth
Add to Calendar Share
Romans 12:1–2

About This Message

In this message, Pastor Jim explores what it means to offer ourselves as "living sacrifices" and how true worship extends far beyond Sunday morning. Drawing from Romans 12, we discover that worship is not just singing — it's the way we live every moment of every day.

Paul urges believers not to conform to the patterns of this world, but to be transformed by the renewing of their minds. This transformation is the heart of worship — a daily surrender that changes how we think, how we act, and how we love the people around us.
