Discipleship

We have many opportunities to grow in Christ. Even for kids, we know the Bible tells us in Proverbs to "train a child up in the way they should go" and in Isaiah "precept upon precept, line upon line". In doing so, we build Christ followers early from the ground up. But, trusting Christ is just the beginning, we must then grow and constantly be maturing into the person Christ has called and planned for us to be. This can only be done if we possess the knowledge and a desire to give away what we have learned to make other disciples. At FBC Concord, this is done thru Sunday school, for all ages, and youth as we grow older all the way up to adults on Wednesday nights as a church and also in specialized groups like Men and Women's Bible Study on Sunday Nights. At every turn we seek to make disciples. Come join us and grow as a brothers and sisters in Christ.
