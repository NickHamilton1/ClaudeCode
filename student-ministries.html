Welcome to Revolution Student Ministries (RSM) here at First Baptist Church of Concord! We're glad that you're interested in finding out more about our student ministry!

Our student ministry has the mission to love God, love others, and make disciples. We can accomplish this mission by learning and serving together.

If you're in 6th - 12th grade, then this is the place for you!

We strive to work alongside parents to develop each student into a disciple of Christ, all while having a great time!  We're a part of a growing fellowship of believers here at First Baptist, and would love to have you join us.
